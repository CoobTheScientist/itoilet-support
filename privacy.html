Overview

iToilet ("we", "us") is developed by Yacob Ahmad. This policy explains what information the iToilet iOS app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We collect only what is needed to run a community bathroom map. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Information We Collect

What Is Public

iToilet is a community map. The following is visible to all users of the app: bathrooms you add (including their location and any notes), your reviews and ratings, your display name, and your territory counts on leaderboards. Do not include personal information in review text or bathroom notes.

Where Data Is Stored

All app data is stored in Apple's iCloud / CloudKit public database in Apple's data centres, protected by Apple's security practices. A copy of your own profile is cached on your device so the app works offline. We do not operate our own servers and no data is shared with any third party other than Apple as our infrastructure provider.

How We Use Information

  • To operate the map, reviews, and territory features.
  • To aggregate ratings (for example, average cleanliness of a bathroom).
  • To moderate content, respond to reports, and keep the community safe.
  • To respond to your support requests.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or tracking across other apps or websites.

Your Choices and Rights

  • Location: You can deny or revoke location access in iOS Settings at any time. The app still works — pan the map and tap refresh to search an area.
  • Display name: Change it in Profile → Edit Display Name.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Delete Account permanently removes your profile, reviews, and territory marks. Bathrooms you added stay on the map with your name removed. You may also revoke iToilet's access to your Apple ID in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Sign in with Apple.
  • Access or correction: Email yacob.ahmad98@gmail.com and we will provide or correct the data associated with your account.

If you are in the EU/EEA, UK, California, or another jurisdiction with data-protection rights, you may exercise those rights by contacting us. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA.

Data Retention

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account it is removed from our database immediately. Reports you filed are deleted with your account; content you reported may be retained for moderation. Apple may keep backups for a limited period per its own policies.

Children

iToilet is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored in Apple's CloudKit. Authentication uses Sign in with Apple; we never store passwords. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so please do not post sensitive information in the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
