About PottyPass

PottyPass is a community-built map of public bathrooms for iPhone and iPad. Find a restroom near you, read honest reviews on cleanliness, toilet paper quality, accessibility and more, add bathrooms you discover, and "mark your territory" to track the cities, states and countries you've conquered.

Reporting Content

PottyPass has zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour. If you see a review that is offensive, spam, or inaccurate, tap the ⋯ menu on the review and choose Report Review, or Block the user to hide everything they post. Reports are reviewed and acted on within 24 hours. You can also email us with the details.

Deleting Your Account

Open the app, go to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, reviews, and territory marks. Bathrooms you submitted remain on the map without your name so the community still benefits. If you can't access the app, email us from the address associated with your Apple ID and we'll delete it for you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does PottyPass ask for my location?

To show bathrooms near you, calculate distances, and let you add a bathroom at your current spot. Location is only used while the app is open and is never used for advertising or sold. You can browse the map without granting location access by panning and tapping refresh.

Why doesn't my bathroom appear on the map yet?

New bathrooms are reviewed by a moderator before they go live to keep the map accurate. This usually takes less than a day.

Do I need an account?

No. You can browse bathrooms and read reviews without signing in. An account (via Sign in with Apple) is only needed to add bathrooms, write reviews, or mark territory.

What are tips?

Optional one-time purchases that support development and server costs. They don't unlock any features. Purchases are handled by Apple; refund requests go through Apple's Report a Problem page.
